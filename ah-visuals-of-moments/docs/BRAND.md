# AH Visuals of Moments — Brand Guide

## Brand name
Use the text name consistently as:

**AH Visuals of Moments**

The graphic mark may present the initials as a stylized **AH** monogram.

Do not introduce variants such as `A&H` or `A-H` without approval.

## Logo
The current logo consists of:
- a large stylized `A` and `H`;
- a thin horizontal connection between the letters;
- the uppercase descriptor `VISUALS OF MOMENTS`;
- generous letter spacing;
- a dark background with a warm light mark.

Treat the logo as a protected original asset. Do not redraw, distort, crop or recolor it without approval.

## Brand character
The brand should feel:
- artistic;
- calm;
- minimal;
- premium without being ostentatious;
- personal;
- editorial;
- contemporary;
- connected to photography and fashion.

The website should combine a modern photography gallery with a restrained fashion catalogue.

## Visual priorities
1. Photography is the primary visual element.
2. Stories provide emotional and cultural context.
3. T-shirts are physical objects carrying a photographed moment.
4. The interface should remain visually quiet.
5. QR codes should feel integrated, not technical clutter.

## Theme direction

### Dark theme
Use near-black backgrounds, graphite surfaces, warm off-white text, muted beige or warm-gray secondary text and subtle borders.

### Light theme
Use warm white or soft ivory backgrounds, near-black text, restrained beige, sand, warm-gray or muted-khaki accents and generous negative space.

## Preliminary palette
- near black;
- warm white;
- ivory;
- graphite;
- muted beige;
- light brown;
- muted khaki.

Avoid bright saturated UI colors, generic blue SaaS accents, strong gradients, neon effects and excessive shadows.

## Typography
Use a two-family system:

### Display
A refined high-contrast serif for collection titles, story titles and editorial statements.

### Interface
A clean sans-serif for navigation, body copy, metadata, buttons, coordinates and controls.

Uppercase labels may use increased letter spacing. Mobile readability takes priority over decorative styling.

## Layout direction
Prefer:
- large photography;
- asymmetrical editorial grids where appropriate;
- generous whitespace;
- clear hierarchy;
- minimal interface chrome;
- strong mobile layouts;
- restrained borders.

Avoid repetitive generic card grids with identical visual weight.

## Motion
Use motion only where it improves understanding:
- image transitions;
- gallery navigation;
- QR modal opening;
- theme transitions;
- understated hover feedback.

Avoid continuous decorative animation, dramatic page transitions, excessive parallax and motion that delays reading. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Image treatment
- Preserve the photographer's intended composition.
- Do not apply filters that alter the original image.
- Use responsive high-quality sources.
- Provide meaningful alternative text.
- Keep the original photograph visually distinct from T-shirt mockups.

## Content tone
Stories should feel personal, sincere, observant and emotionally restrained.

Avoid aggressive commercial language, artificial luxury claims and generic advertising slogans.

## Restrictions
Do not:
- modify the logo without approval;
- use unapproved brand-name variants;
- make the site resemble a generic marketplace;
- show prices or shopping controls in MVP;
- let decorative effects compete with photography;
- rewrite the photographer's stories without review.
